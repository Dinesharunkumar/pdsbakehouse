Our story

Made for shared moments

PD's Bake House began in 2019 with a simple idea: make every celebration feel warmer, sweeter and more personal. What started as a small baking passion soon grew into a neighborhood bakery loved for thoughtful cakes, rich brownies and joyful gifting treats.

Thoughtful bakes, every day.

At PD's Bake House, every cake and cookie is baked in small batches to preserve freshness, texture and flavor. We believe good desserts should feel personal, warm and celebratory without losing their simplicity.

Whether it is an intimate family gathering, a custom celebration cake or a gift box for someone special, our approach stays the same: use quality ingredients, finish with care and make each order feel memorable from the first look to the final bite.

Small-batch fresh

We keep things intentionally limited so each order is crafted with care and consistency.

Clean ingredients

We focus on thoughtful flavor pairings, high-quality dairy and balanced textures.

Personal finishing

Messaged cakes, thoughtful gifting and custom details are part of our everyday experience.

Freshly baked joy, beautifully crafted since 2019.
