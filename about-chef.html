The baker behind it

Chef PD

Behind PD's Bake House is a pastry craft rooted in patience, detail and the joy of creating desserts that feel like part of the celebration itself.

Crafted with heart

Turning sweet ideas into celebration moments.

Chef PD blends pastry technique with a personal, artistic eye. Every cake is designed to feel warm, memorable and deeply personal — whether it is a birthday centrepiece or a thoughtfully packed gift.

The result is a menu that feels both elegant and comforting: refined enough for special occasions, playful enough for joyful family moments.

Professional milestone

Lavonne Academy: a mark of serious pastry training.

Chef PD completed a baking certification at Lavonne Academy of Baking Science and Pastry Arts, a well-regarded name in the Indian baking and pastry education space.

Lavonne is known for combining technical discipline, creative execution and bakery professionalism — the kind of training that helps a chef understand not just taste, but structure, finish, balance and consistency.

Why it adds value

Better technique, stronger details, better desserts.

Professional training adds more than technique — it builds confidence, consistency and a deeper understanding of ingredients, textures and presentation.

The result is a chef who understands both the science of baking and the emotion behind a memorable dessert.
